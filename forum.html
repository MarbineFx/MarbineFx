Forum Rules Peraturan Forum

1 Explain your setup. Avoid empty “BUY NOW” or “SELL NOW” spam. Terangkan setup anda. Elakkan spam “BUY NOW” atau “SELL NOW”.
2 Never share passwords, API keys or personal financial information. Jangan kongsi kata laluan, API key atau maklumat kewangan peribadi.
3 Respect other traders. Critique the idea, not the person. Hormati trader lain. Kritik idea, bukan individu.
4 Forum posts are discussion, not guaranteed signals. Post forum ialah perbincangan, bukan signal yang dijamin.
MarbineFX Tip

A useful post includes timeframe, direction, key level, invalidation and what would change your bias. Post yang berguna ada timeframe, arah, level penting, invalidation dan apa yang boleh mengubah bias.
